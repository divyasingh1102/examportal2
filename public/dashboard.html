Exam Guidelines & Integrity Rules

Please review the anti-cheat guidelines carefully before beginning:

  • 1
    Timed Session: Once started, the countdown timer cannot be paused. When it reaches 0:00, your exam will auto-submit.
  • 2
    Dynamic Shuffling: Question sequence and option choices are randomized uniquely for your attempt.
  • 3
    Tab-Switch Detection: Browser Page Visibility is strictly enforced.
    • 1st tab switch / blur: Warning 1 of 2
    • 2nd tab switch / blur: Warning 2 of 2
    • 3rd tab switch / blur: Immediate Test Termination & Auto-Submit
  • 4
    Prohibited Actions: Right-clicking, copying questions, and switching applications are strictly disabled.
ℹ️ Your test results, question breakdown, and score will be generated instantly upon submission.
